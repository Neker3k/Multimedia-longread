03 / Sokol · Kazakhstan

Your first start costs more than an entry fee

At Sokol, you can try circuit racing through a track day or Time Attack. To enter a round, you need your own car, a FAMS licence, and a medical certificate. Car preparation, repairs, and travel cost extra.

55 000 ₸Time Attack round entry, 2026 season
35 000 ₸separate practice day on the full circuit

04 / A local example

The Cobalt can make the grid too

The contrast is almost comic: a familiar city sedan now lines up on a circuit.

In October 2025, Allur and Sokol held Cobalt Challenge, a test event for specially prepared Chevrolet Cobalts. The initiative was intended as the basis for an accessible single-make circuit class, and organisers announced plans to launch Cobalt Cup in 2026. The full national championship has not started. The project now offers track days and paid rentals of race-prepared cars for people who want to try the circuit.

05 / When the scale grows

From a local start to Super Trofeo

ArtLine Kazakhstan competes in Lamborghini Super Trofeo. In 2025, Shota Abkhazava and Egor Oruzhев won the World Final in the Pro-Am class.

Published season estimate
≈300 000 €

Estimate for one driver for a full season, given in a 2024 interview. This is not ArtLine disclosed budget.

If a sponsor funds the entire programme, the driver may not have to pay out of pocket. The agreement should specify what it covers: car, team, tyres, repairs, travel, and entry fees.

The answer

You can start with a track day. To continue, you need resources for a car, preparation, and regular race entries.

Between your first lap and a professional series, you need more than speed and talent: access to a circuit, a budget, and support.
